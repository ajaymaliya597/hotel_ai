Good to know

A few helpful answers.

Need something else? Open Nova for help with the supported booking and cancellation workflows.

How can I see room prices and amenities?

Choose your dates and guest count, then search availability. Room descriptions, capacity and nightly rates are returned by the existing booking system. Confirm any property-specific service not listed there directly with the hotel.

Can I use Nova without a microphone?

Yes. Open the AI assistant and type into the conversation box. Typed messages use the same Nova chat workflow and do not require microphone or speaker access.

What languages can I use with Nova?

The assistant interface supports English, Hindi, Hinglish, Tamil and Telugu options. Auto-detect is also available; voice playback depends on the voices available to your browser and network connection.

How do I look up or cancel a booking?

Open My Bookings and enter the guest name and mobile number used for the reservation. Matching bookings are shown by the existing booking service, and confirmed reservations can be cancelled through its current flow.

When is a booking confirmed?

Follow the confirmation returned by the booking service and save the booking code. Nova's chat responses and this page do not replace a successful server-side booking response.
